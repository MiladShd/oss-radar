# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
The user selected a portfolio demonstration of data science results, reported with executive clarity.

## Product Purpose
Demonstrate whether download-history models add measurable predictive value, and what the evidence supports. Preserve dependency monitoring as a practical application.

## Capabilities and Constraints
FastAPI, a static browser interface, BigQuery in production and DuckDB locally. Existing technical explorer, audit, models, activity, and validation remain available. A research refit is not a served-model evaluation. Download volume is not unique adoption. Composite risk is not an event probability. Never invent prospective performance, ROI, or avoided incidents.

## Evidence on Hand
Stored pipeline records, predictions, origin features, download history, source metadata, and research backtests. Historical prediction timing may precede completion but follow the original outcome-window start; exclude these from the strict prospective scorecard. Portfolio evidence should make that gap visible.

## Product Principles
Report the question, comparison, measured result, limitation, and next experiment. Show failed hypotheses alongside positive results. Keep operational health separate from statistical performance. Prefer interpretable outcomes over decorative scores.
