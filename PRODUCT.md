# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
The user selected a portfolio demonstration of data science results, reported with executive clarity.

## Product Purpose
Keep the existing monitoring website focused on useful observed changes and concrete findings. A separate evidence report demonstrates whether download-history models add measurable predictive value, and what the evidence supports.

## Capabilities and Constraints
FastAPI, a static browser interface, BigQuery in production and DuckDB locally. The existing application remains at `/`; the evidence report is a new `/evidence` page in the established design system. Audit, models, activity, and validation remain available; technical views use disclosure navigation. A research refit is not a served-model evaluation. Download volume is not unique adoption. Composite risk is not an event probability. Never invent prospective performance, ROI, or avoided incidents.

## Evidence on Hand
Stored pipeline records, predictions, origin features, download history, source metadata, and research backtests. Historical prediction timing may precede completion but follow the original outcome-window start; exclude these from the strict prospective scorecard. Portfolio evidence should make that gap visible.

## Product Principles
Report the question, comparison, measured result, limitation, and next experiment. Show failed hypotheses alongside positive results. Keep operational health separate from statistical performance. Prefer interpretable outcomes over decorative scores.
