Latest experiment · held-out research diagnostic

This diagnostic retrains a model for its evaluation. It does not measure the exact model serving today, and its overlapping outcome windows do not establish independent forecasting performance.

R² compares squared error with predicting the evaluation-set mean. Rank correlation measures ordering, not the accuracy of predicted growth. Neither is an accuracy percentage.

What was tested

Question and outcome

Predict the change between the next 70 days and the previous 70 days of downloads, using a trailing 28-day smoother. Features describe download levels, trend, and volatility. Downloads measure activity, not unique users or business adoption.

Evaluation and its limit

Origin dates are ordered into train, validation, and test partitions. Their 70-day outcome windows overlap, with no embargo. This is research evidence about ranking, not proof that a model issued today will forecast accurately.

What would strengthen the claim?

Freeze model, origin date, input data version, and prediction before the forecast window begins. After 70 days, compare the exact served predictions with no-change and calibrated persistence baselines. Repeat across non-overlapping origin windows; report uncertainty by package and origin. Until then, predictive usefulness remains unestablished.
