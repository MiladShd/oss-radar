# Code of Conduct

OSS Radar welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and points of
view. Be respectful, assume good intent, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Give constructive, specific feedback.
- Respect differing experiences and viewpoints.
- Credit other people's ideas and work.
- Accept correction and repair harm when a conversation goes wrong.
- Protect private information and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or sexualized
  conduct.

This policy applies in repository discussions, pull requests, issues, and other spaces where
someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an immediate public problem, use GitHub's report/block controls. For a confidential report,
contact the repository owner through the profile linked from the repository. Include links,
screenshots, and any context needed to understand the incident.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or
permanently ban someone when behavior puts contributors or the community at risk. Reports will be
handled as privately as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
